WHY HYDROGEN?

Pure water. Measurably better.

In an April 2025 randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial at the University of Novi Sad (HYDRO2EAU, NCT06788015), collegiate athletes who drank hydrogen-infused water for four weeks showed significant improvements in maximum running velocity (P=0.01), velocity at the anaerobic threshold (P=0.04), and self-reported mental and general health scores (P=0.05 and P=0.03). No adverse effects were reported.

HtoEAU® delivers 5 mg/L of dissolved molecular hydrogen - materially higher than the concentration used in the HYDRO2EAU trial. The study was co-funded by The Hydrogen Innovation Company, HtoEAU®'s parent company, and is a preprint (not yet peer-reviewed).
